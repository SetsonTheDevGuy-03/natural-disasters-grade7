Natural Disaster Detectives

Work together, investigate one disaster and present what you discover.

Activity 1: Group investigation

Divide the class into groups. Each group receives one disaster: flood, drought, tornado, earthquake or volcano.

Your fact card must include:

  • Name and definition.
  • How it occurs.
  • Where it occurs.
  • Three effects on people.
  • Three effects on the environment.
  • How communities can prepare.
  • One interesting fact.
  • A Namibia connection where possible.
  • A labelled drawing.

Presentation: Each group gets 2–3 minutes to present its disaster.

Activity 2: Namibia flood scenario

A northern Namibian community experiences several days of heavy rainfall. Water begins covering roads, fields and areas around homes.

  1. What natural disaster is occurring?
  2. What could happen to roads?
  3. What could happen to crops?
  4. How could learners get to school?
  5. What should families do to stay safe?
  6. How could authorities assist the community?

Discussion corner

Question 1: Which natural disaster could have the greatest effect on a Namibian community, and why?
Question 2: Is every natural hazard automatically a disaster? Explain your answer.

Use these as class or small-group discussion questions. They count as a learner interaction feature for the assignment.
